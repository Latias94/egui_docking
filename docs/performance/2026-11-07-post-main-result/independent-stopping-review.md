# Independent stopping challenge after nine post-main experiments

**I recommend stopping this optimization search now and proceeding with the remaining publication qualification.** I found no additional bounded deletion with sufficiently distinct current-source cost evidence to justify another screen. This is a practical judgment about the present workload, implementation and engineering tradeoffs. It is not numerical completion, a lower bound, proof of optimality, or a conclusion that further optimization is impossible.

This changes my earlier recommendation to continue: the two concrete small opportunities identified or subsequently investigated after that challenge have now been implemented and rejected under their own rules. I do not recommend repeating either treatment or using another variant merely to search for favorable code generation.

## Why the latest two results matter

Borrowed topology was a real, small ownership deletion. The direct projection temporarily retained a topology Arc in addition to its two durable result owners; the source-matched diagnostic showed those operations surviving. Its private borrowing implementation preserved output ownership, delayed measurement, raw projection and arithmetic. Nevertheless, complete p50 improved in 26/42 conditions, below 28, and small layout improved in 3/14, below 8. My independent archive review reproduced those numbers, the raw summaries, work equality and complete vectors. There is no basis for an additive confirmation or retention.

Scalar issuance addressed a different observed cost: root-reference allocation and reference-count traffic in current checked mutation. Its implementation used an exact checked unique serial and concrete predecessor metadata, with explicit restoration semantics and a separate restoration witness. The decision records complete p50 18/21/3, affected moves 16/30 against the required 20, small moves 7/10 passing their gate, and candidate/ImGui 29/42 below the required 30. Passing one small subgroup does not rescue the failed affected-workload and inventory gates. Original Arc/Weak authority is restored.

Both are three-gate early screens with work and complete-output checks, not twelve-gate qualification. The scalar treatment also passed its additional restoration witness. Neither failure proves that atomics or allocations are free, or that another owner representation cannot win.

The previous seven decisions remain relevant for their actual mechanisms, rather than as a count:

- Nonempty-source reuse deleted a real redundant content scan, but failed the predeclared resize protection after additive confirmation.
- Equal binary initialization initially looked favorable, but failed complete and small-layout gates on confirmation. Its favorable initial campaign cannot be selected alone.
- Root-build stack reserve, placement-roster borrowing and insertion-aware Tabs COW targeted current located allocation/copy paths and failed their affected-cell gates.
- Release-prefix removal and Live Tabs fact borrowing addressed current per-node defense work and repeated target/reference capture. Both failed their screens and were restored.

The first three treatments completed twelve ordinary gates; the later six followed early screening. None should be described as a twelve-gate winner, and none is retained.

## Current-source challenge

Stopping is not justified by “nine failed.” It is justified by the distinction between the remaining concrete work and the cost of another meaningfully different treatment.

**Authority and candidate ownership.** `operation.rs:628–702` still prepares an independent candidate, authorizes the actual current source and target, executes the checked move, and locally finishes its changed roots. `graph/shared_storage.rs:16–23` copies generational arena handles while sharing payloads; `graph.rs:396–420` and `authority.rs:87–90` retain the canonical storage and normative authority directories. These choices incur work but preserve discardable candidates, staged commands, exact identities, rollback and old outputs. They are not a theoretical requirement to use these particular containers.

Cancelling that fork would require a concrete overlay/undo or different persistent-directory design with read-view, allocation-branch, canonicalization and engine publication implications. It is not another small deletion in an existing owner. The mature persistent-directory treatment already has negative complete results; this does not disprove all alternatives, but it supplies no positive basis for a new directory family now. Such a redesign also does not address the missed resize conditions, which perform no command mutation.

**Repeated checks and issuance.** Current Tabs and edge validators still verify references and rederive target facts (`operation.rs:2895–2967`, `workspace.rs:626–789`). Some checks and temporary ownership are repeated in source; I am not claiming they all became necessary or disappeared. The direct Live Tabs deletion has now been tested. An analogous edge-only recapture treatment is untested, but would mostly transfer the same mechanism to a narrower subgroup with no newly isolated cost basis. Scalar issuance tested the separate, larger issuer-allocation deletion. Neither result supports changing strict authority, error priority or no-op restoration just to reduce apparent work.

There are also small repeated queries, such as the root lookup before and after override validation in `layout.rs:496–503`. Source repetition alone does not establish a distinct surviving cost or a worthwhile screen. One more local query or Arc spelling change, without a new attributable mechanism, is not sufficient reason to continue indefinitely.

**Fresh layout and complete output.** Measurement already writes the same `Vec<NodeResult>` later used by projection. The output owns that node buffer plus split outcomes and materialized per-edge results. Binary measurement gather and numeric inputs use local two-element arrays; the named default scratch Vecs do not each allocate for every binary split. Numeric validation, minimum/maximum composition and the sole constrained executor retain their full semantics.

`docking_cost.rs:197–250` gathers actual fresh leaf inputs and calls layout independently for every root; lines 329–350 build a fresh owned common vector; lines 441–468 keep capture through export inside the original timer. Skipping input collection, returning borrowed exports, omitting rich fields, making results lazy or moving work outside the timer would change the declared operation. The current representation still has initialization and copy overhead that another design might reduce; the complete-output contract does not prove that every current store is minimal.

**Compiled projection events.** This remains a legitimate unmeasured architectural direction. It could remove the temporary continuation stack. However, `layout.rs:959–969,1190–1261` retains the exact parent cursor, original compressed splitter thickness and descendant-before-gap order. Compiling that control into graph-owned events/links adds retained metadata and root-completion work; raw DAG occurrences still need correct per-parent traversal and last writes. Reconstructing a cursor from rectangle width introduces different subtraction/rounding behavior; using an existing maximum avoids that subtraction but does not remove the remaining state problem.

Current evidence locates stack allocation/disposal without isolating a benefit sufficient to outweigh those changes. A failed container or fused-transition treatment does not refute compiled events, but neither makes this larger design the next mandatory experiment. Its root-build and output-lifetime tradeoffs are concrete, while its complete-operation advantage remains unsupported.

**Cross-root scratch.** An explicit operation-local scratch owner could reuse the continuation stack across views, and general nonbinary layouts could reuse more numeric capacity. On this matrix, binary numeric arrays already avoid that heap work. The frozen caller invokes stateless `project_root` per root; honest reuse requires caller/API migration, not merely changing an internal Vec. Hidden global/thread-local or workspace caches would be an inappropriate seam. This is a possible separately scoped API experiment, not an unperformed small production deletion under the current comparison boundary.

**Transient split rows and root completion.** `root_structure.rs:178–183` writes a provisional split roster, then `finish_directory:210–220` clears and refills that same buffer in identity order. A local scalar could avoid provisional stores. It would not remove the final owner, its capacity or the single identity sort, and current location evidence does not isolate those stores. Removing the final directory would instead change iteration/lookup tradeoffs. There is no duplicate root sort to delete, and it would be misleading to transfer large fixture-build samples to timed mutation.

These residual hypotheses are deferred by scope, leverage and evidence, not declared experimentally false. No current finding requires another broad profile, a tenth treatment or another confirmation of already failed candidates.

## Recommendation and remaining obligations

End the present runtime search, retain `0e441c8`, preserve every negative campaign and the intermediate reviews that caused further investigation, and publish the result with the twelve misses clearly stated. The existing matched diagnostic is adequate for this practical decision; running another profile without a distinct question would not itself resolve a remaining design choice.

A future reopening should identify a genuinely different source-level deletion, its exact contract and caller scope, its new negative costs, and the cheapest diagnostic that distinguishes it from previous treatments. That possibility remains open. This review does not claim a Rust/ownership floor, an unavoidable ratio, numerical optimality, or impossibility of all 42 passing.

The runtime-search stop does **not** complete publication. The existing six green jobs qualify `c7508ee`; final push and six-job CI qualification of the exact publication head remain mandatory and unfinished.
