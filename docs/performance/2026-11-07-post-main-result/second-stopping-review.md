These are not declarations that small savings cannot matter. They distinguish their known deleted work, retained costs, and unmeasured implementation risk. None has a new source-matched finding strong enough to outweigh those costs now. The fact that an edit is easy to express, or that a related edit failed, does not by itself establish its benefit or rejection.

Compiled projection events are a different architecture: they exchange dynamic continuation work for event production, retained schedule data, and rebuild work after edge edits. They must preserve parent solve/child bounds order, descendants-before-following-gap errors, original splitter thickness, central propagation, and raw-DAG revisits/last writes. There is no present net-cost evidence covering those new owners and phases. This is not a bounded deletion ready to screen simply because continuation frames appear in the profile.

Cross-root scratch migration likewise needs a caller/API seam or hidden persistent storage. The unchanged formal caller projects roots separately. Binary constraints, weights and sizes already use local arrays; sixteen leaves do not imply n-ary scratch allocation. A scene-level batch or reusable workspace scratch may help another workload, but cannot be credited to this matrix without a separate applicable measurement and reentrancy/failure-lifetime design.

Fresh rich output is not an optional cost. Node and packed child/split results remain complete, independently owned and valid after workspace destruction or later edits. The common export is fresh and complete. Replacing those with prior-frame values, a lazy facade, cached leaf answers, or partial results would change the measured operation. Independent candidates, MRU, old references and atomic rollback also cannot be removed by mutation-and-undo shortcuts.

The earlier rejected persistent-store and numerical variants remain rejected specific designs. Changing reserve constants, counter schemes, object layout or combining previously rejected patches without a new mechanism-specific reason is not the evidence needed to reopen them.
